Web Edition

An HTML5 remake of the classic Alex the Allegator 4, created in 2003 by Johan Peitz of Free Lunch Design. Lola has been captured by evil humans who want to make a bag of her: guide Alex through the jungle and save her! This edition has the whole game, all 12 levels, both guardians, the intro and the ending, and was made with melonJS, a free HTML5 game engine.

It is a modernized Alex 4 that stays true to the original: the same levels, the same four-color graphics, music and rules, at the original's pace. What it adds is game feel: Alex squashes as he lands, the action freezes for a split second on every stomp and every blow to a guardian, the screen shakes on the big impacts, pickups sparkle, dust flies, the ball leaves a trail, the jungle sways, the water ripples and the screen breaks into big pixels between scenes.

Click or tap the game to turn the sound on.

How to play

Every level has an exit that Alex must find. On the way, eat stars and cherries for points, hearts for energy and eggs to spit at the enemies. Jump on an enemy to knock it out, or step on a roll block to turn into a ball and smash through ice blocks.

Keyboard
← → move, X, Space or ↑ jump, Z, C or Enter spit, P pause, Esc back to the menu
Gamepad
stick or D-pad to move, A jump, B or X spit, Start pause, Select back to the menu
Menus
arrows and Enter, or click and tap; Esc, a click or a tap skips the intro

Tip from the original manual: try to collect all the stars and cherries on every level.
